History

Whitehall WWR Co. began in 2017 with a group of church youth, one raft, and a love for the river. What started as weekend trips for family and neighbors grew into a guided adventure company that now welcomes thousands of guests each season.

Our founders’ values haven’t changed. We still hire guides who know every bend in the river and still treat every guest like part of the crew.
