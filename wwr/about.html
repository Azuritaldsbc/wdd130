History

Whitehall WWR Co. began in 2017 with a group of church youth, one raft, and a love for the river. What started as weekend trips for family and neighbors grew into a guided adventure company that now welcomes thousands of guests each season. Our founders’ values haven’t changed. We still hire guides who know every bend in the river and still treat every guest like part of the crew.

Adventure Awaits You!

Ready to trade your screen for spray in your face? From gentle family floats to heart-pounding Class IV rapids, we have a trip for every age and skill level. Our certified guides provide all the gear, training, and encouragement you need. All you have to bring is your sense of adventure. Grab your friends, book your trip, and let the river do the rest!
